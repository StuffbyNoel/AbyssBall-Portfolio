THE CONCEPT

Abyss Ball is my very first project built in Unity—a fast-paced mobile hypercasual game where players guide a bouncing ball down a vertical tower by rotating stacked disks, dropping through narrow gaps while steering clear of red danger zones.

Built end-to-end for Android, the project served as my hands-on introduction to Unity physics, touch-driven rotation controls, level progression, score tracking, and complete game-state UI including main menus, pause overlays, and instant restart loops.
